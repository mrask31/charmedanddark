# Gifts for Smut Readers: The Smutty Good Girl Guide

Buying a book for a smut reader can be surprisingly difficult. They may already own it, have it waiting on their e-reader, or have strong opinions about a particular trope. A useful reading accessory lets you celebrate their favorite pastime without guessing what belongs on their bookshelf.

Our [Smutty Good Girl collection](/collections/smutty-good-girl), or S.G.G., brings a playful gothic sensibility to those everyday reading essentials. Think book totes, drinkware, a reading journal, and comfortable touches for the corner where “one more chapter” becomes an evening.

S.G.G. is for adult readers who enjoy sexually explicit books and unapologetically questionable fictional tastes. It is about what they like on the page, not a single genre: erotica, contemporary stories, fantasy, and dark romance can all have a place on that shelf. Dark romance is one possibility, not the definition.

Here is how to choose a gift that celebrates their reading habits without guessing their next five-star read.

## Start with how they read

A thoughtful gift starts with a habit. Does your reader carry a paperback everywhere, curl up with an e-reader, or bring a whole stack home from the bookstore?

For someone who reads on the go, a tote is a useful place to start. For a homebody, consider a mug or a comfortable blanket. For someone who remembers every fictional relationship but forgets which book came next, a reading journal gives them a place for ratings, favorite tropes, and the next title to try.

Also consider where they will use the gift. Bold Smutty Good Girl lettering may be perfect for their private reading nook or book-club outing. If you are unsure whether they would carry it to work, ask about their style before choosing.

## For the reader who settles in with a drink

A reading mug is an easy choice when you know their coffee or tea ritual better than their to-be-read list. The Reading Fuel Mug in the S.G.G. collection offers a bookish detail they can use outside reading time, too.

Build a small gift around one habit: a mug with their preferred tea, or a reading journal with a pen they enjoy using. You do not need a large bundle to make the gift feel considered. Choose the central piece first, then add something personal within your budget.

## For a darker reading corner

Start with a comfortable seat and enough task lighting to read without straining. Add a blanket within reach, a place for a drink, and one decorative detail. A reading corner does not need a complete room makeover.

Our [Burn After Reading Cinnamon Vanilla Candle](/shop/smutty-good-girl-burn-after-reading-cinnamon-vanilla-candle) adds a warm scent to the setting. Check whether your recipient enjoys scented candles before choosing one. Keep a lit candle on a stable, heat-resistant surface away from books, blankets, children, and pets, and extinguish it before leaving or falling asleep.

For a coordinated look, explore the blanket and Reading Nook Pillow in the [S.G.G. collection](/collections/smutty-good-girl). Choose one accent that works with the room rather than feeling you need every matching piece.

## A few questions before you choose

**What can I buy a smut reader besides books?**

Start with something they will use: a tote, mug, reading journal, or blanket. Match the choice to where and how they read.

**Is S.G.G. only for dark romance readers?**

No. S.G.G. celebrates adult readers of sexually explicit stories across genres. Dark romance describes a particular kind of story and its themes; it does not describe every book with explicit scenes. Match a gift to the individual reader, without assuming their preferred genre or fictional tastes.
